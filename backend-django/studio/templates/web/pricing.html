How do I pay?

Sign in with Google, choose a pack in Add Credits, enter your phone number, and continue to PayU. PayU displays the payment options available for your checkout and handles your payment details securely.

When do credits appear?

Credits are added after the server verifies a successful live payment. Your payment status appears under Your web purchases. Pending, cancelled and failed payments do not grant credits.

How much does a creation cost?

The credit cost depends on the model, resolution, duration and audio settings. Your studio shows an estimate before you create and confirms the final credit quote before submission.

Can I use my Android account?

Yes. Use the same Google account to share credits and cloud creations. Web pack prices are separate from Google Play prices; purchasing on one platform updates the same wallet.

Review the final amount and applicable charges on PayU before paying. Terms of Service apply.
